# Privacy Policy for Kairos

**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

Kairos ("we", "our", or "the app") is a privacy-first, local-first clock and time instrument developed by Samyukt.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Kairos is built on a strict local-first philosophy:
- We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.
- We do **not** collect unique device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data.
- We do **not** use tracking cookies or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

### 2. Local-First Storage
All alarms, timers, moments, settings, and time zone preferences created within Kairos are stored exclusively in your device's local sandbox storage. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, all stored data is deleted.

### 3. Permissions
Kairos requests only the minimum OS permissions necessary for clock and alarm functionality:
- **Notifications / Alarms**: Used strictly to trigger scheduled countdowns and alarms when the app is in the background.
- No network, contact, camera, microphone, or storage permissions are required for core usage.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Because Kairos does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.
